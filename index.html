About the data
  • Shuttle times were transcribed from the University timetable for this prototype. The app data does not update automatically.
  • Route C switches between its in-semester and out-of-semester timetable using the Semester dates in the attached 2026 and 2027 student calendars. Breaks and exams within a Semester date range use the in-semester timetable.
  • The source does not clearly state whether routes A, B and C operate on public holidays or every weekday. Check the official timetable before travelling; results are not a guarantee of service.
  • Live bus locations and delays are not shown. Camden routes are excluded.
Source: University of Sydney Campus shuttle bus timetables
